Features

Everything after the meeting, done.

Joins every call

Connect your calendar once. Nimbus shows up, records and transcribes, no bots to invite by hand.

Decisions & tasks

Not a wall of text: a short summary, the decisions made and who owes what by when.

Search all meetings

Ask "what did we decide about pricing?" and jump straight to that moment in the call.

30+ languages

Mixed-language calls are fine. Summaries arrive in the language your team reads.

Sends itself

Summaries go to Slack, email or Notion automatically, right after the call ends.

Private by default

Recordings are encrypted and only shared with people who were in the meeting.
